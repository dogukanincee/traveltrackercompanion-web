Subscription & Billing Policy

Version 1.0 Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Subscription Tiers

TravelTrackerCompanion offers Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime Premium tiers. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the current billing cycle.

2. Free Trials

Free trial periods unlock full Premium access. If you do not cancel before the trial period expires, your store account will be billed for the chosen subscription plan.

3. Managing & Canceling Subscriptions

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your iOS App Store or Google Play Store account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the conclusion of the active billing period.
