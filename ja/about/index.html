Version 1.0 Developer Studio: Travel Tracker Companion Studio

1. What is TravelTrackerCompanion?

TravelTrackerCompanion is a cross-platform (Android and iOS) travel logbook, expense tracker, document vault, and expedition companion engineered with complete offline-first resilience.

2. Product Pillars

2.1 Local-First Architecture

Your travel data, photos, expenses, and vault documents are stored locally in SQLDelight and platform Keychains (AES-256) on your device. You are never forced to create a cloud account.

2.2 Real-Time Travel Context

Integrated APIs provide timely context for your trips: flight tracking via OpenSky Network / AeroDataBox, weather via Open-Meteo, and currency conversion via ExchangeRate-API.

2.3 Geospatial Mapping

Vector maps rendered via MapLibre GL SDK combined with geoBoundaries administrative boundary geometry allow tracking visited countries, cities, and photo timelines without selling location telemetry.
