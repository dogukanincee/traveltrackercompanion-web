Account & Data Deletion Policy

Version 1.0 Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. On-Device Local Data Deletion

Because TravelTrackerCompanion stores trip history, expense ledgers, and document vaults locally on your device, triggering Delete Account & Data inside Settings > Danger Zone immediately wipes your local SQLite database and platform Keychains (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

2. Entitlement & Cloud Session Purge

If you signed in with Google or Apple, account deletion revokes active Supabase auth sessions, deletes associated cloud entitlement records, and permanently purges user profile data. Purchase transaction records are retained up to 7 years solely as required for tax compliance.

3. Data Export Before Deletion

On-device deletion is permanent and irreversible. We strongly recommend using the Export My Data feature prior to initiating deletion if you wish to retain a copy of your travel history.
