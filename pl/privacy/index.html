Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 Effective Date: October 5, 2026 GDPR & CCPA Compliant

1. Privacy-First Architecture

TravelTrackerCompanion ("the App", "we", "us") is engineered around a local-first, privacy-by-design philosophy. Your personal travel history, expense ledgers, logbook notes, and vault documents remain stored locally on your physical device by default.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Trip Data & Documents

All trips, checklists, notes, expenses, and document vault items are stored in encrypted local databases (SQLDelight) and platform Keychains (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore with AES-256). We do not collect or monitor your trip contents.

2.2 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Transactions are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We receive only non-sensitive entitlement tokens and transaction identifiers required to unlock Premium features.

2.3 Diagnostic Data & Crash Reports

If you explicitly enable crash reporting in Settings, anonymous technical diagnostic telemetry (crash stack traces, OS version, device model) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry SDK to improve app stability. Diagnostic data contains no trip details or personal credentials.

3. Third-Party Service Requests

To provide real-time travel context, the App sends minimal query text (such as flight numbers or city names) directly to external travel providers:

  • OpenSky Network / AeroDataBox: Flight position queries
  • Open-Meteo: Weather forecasts
  • ExchangeRate-API: Currency exchange conversions
  • geoBoundaries: Country boundary geometry data
  • Pexels API: Destination photography

4. Data Rights & Retention

Because your data resides on your device, deleting a trip or uninstalling the App permanently removes your local records. You can export your data at any time via the in-app Data Export feature.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, contact us at support@traveltrackercompanion.app.
