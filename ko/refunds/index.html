Refund Policy

Version 1.0 Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Store-Managed Purchases

All in-app purchases and subscriptions for TravelTrackerCompanion are billed and managed directly through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not process payment transactions directly.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow your platform store's official procedure:

  • Apple App Store: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play Store: Visit Google Play Refund Support

3. Entitlement Revocation

When a refund is approved by Apple or Google, Premium entitlements associated with the purchase are automatically revoked upon store notification.
