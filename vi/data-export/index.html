Data Export & Portability

Version 1.0 Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Export Formats

In accordance with GDPR Article 20 and CCPA data portability rights, TravelTrackerCompanion enables you to export your complete travel history in standard, machine-readable formats:

  • JSON Export: Structured export containing complete trip metadata, cities, checklists, and expenses.
  • CSV Export: Tabular spreadsheet export for expense ledgers and trip dates.

2. How to Export

Open Settings > Your Travel Data inside the application, select Export My Data, and choose your preferred destination (email, local file storage, or cloud drive).
